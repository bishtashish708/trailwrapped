# Privacy

TrailWrapped is built so your activity data stays on your own machine.

- There's no hosted service run by this project that stores your data
- Activities are kept in a local SQLite database, not sent anywhere
- The only external network calls are the ones you initiate yourself, like authenticating with Strava to pull your own activities
- No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry baked into the app

If you self-host this on your own server, the same applies, your data stays wherever you put it. What happens after you export and share a recap card is up to you.

If you find something in the code that contradicts this, please open an issue, that's a bug.
